Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

What Colette Is

Colette is an AI-powered language learning companion. You learn through natural conversation with AI tutors who adapt to your level and interests. We take your privacy seriously — here's exactly how we handle your data.

What We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and language preferences. Used to personalize your learning experience.
  • Conversation data: Your chat messages with AI tutors. Used to provide contextual responses and track your progress. Stored encrypted.
  • Learning progress: Vocabulary learned, conversation history, proficiency level. Used to adapt lesson difficulty and track your improvement.
  • Device information: Device type and push notification tokens. Used solely to deliver lesson reminders if you opt in.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't show you ads.
  • We don't share your conversations with third parties for marketing.
  • We don't use your conversations to train AI models beyond improving your personal experience.
  • We don't track you across other websites or apps.

AI & Conversations

Your conversations with Colette's AI tutors are processed by our AI provider to generate responses. Conversations are used to maintain context within your learning sessions. Your conversation data is not used to train general AI models. You can delete your conversation history at any time.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely with encryption at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices. Backups are encrypted and retained for 30 days.

Third-Party Services

  • AI providers: Process your conversations to generate tutor responses (no long-term data retention)
  • Hosting: Vercel for web hosting
  • Analytics: Anonymous, aggregate usage analytics only

Your Rights

  • Access: View all your data within the app.
  • Export: Download your conversation history and learning data.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data permanently. This cannot be undone.
  • Correction: Update your profile information at any time.

EU/EEA users have additional rights under GDPR. California users have rights under CCPA. We don't sell personal information.

Children

Colette is not intended for users under 13 years of age (16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification.
